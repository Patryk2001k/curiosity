# Support & Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Curiosity is committed to providing prompt, professional assistance for all applications published on the Atlassian Marketplace.

---

## Contact Channels

* **Email Support:** `support@curiosity-apps.com` *(lub Twój adres e-mail)*
* **Issue Tracker / Portal:** [Submit a Ticket via GitHub / Service Desk](https://github.com/)
* **Supported Products:** SBAM — Bulk Attachments for Jira (Download & Delete)

---

## Business Hours and Response Times

| Priority Level | Description | Target Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | Core functionality unavailable; operations blocked | Within 8 business hours |
| **Standard** | General inquiry, minor defect, configuration assistance | Within 24 business hours |

* **Standard Business Hours:** Monday through Friday, 09:00 – 17:00 CET.
* **Exclusions:** Official public holidays.

---

## Scope of Support

### Covered:
* Troubleshooting application errors and unexpected behavior.
* Assistance with initial configuration and permission troubleshooting.
* Compatibility updates for Atlassian Jira Cloud releases.

### Not Covered:
* Custom Jira workflow development unrelated to Curiosity apps.
* General administration of third-party Jira apps.
