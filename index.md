## Featured Products

### SBAM — Bulk Attachments for Jira (Download & Delete)
A comprehensive solution for Jira Cloud users and administrators:
- **Smart Anti-Junk Filter:** Automatically detects and filters out email signature images and small badges.
- **Client-Side ZIP Downloader:** Parallel multi-stream downloads with project, issue, and category folder structuring.
- **Virtual Trash Vault:** Safe soft-deletion with configurable retention policies and 3-Tier RBAC.
- **Cross-Project Navigator:** Full JQL query support and folder accordion views.
